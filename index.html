MOONSSH · IPHONE & IPAD

Your terminal, within reach.

MoonSSH connects to the SSH servers you choose. Use a regular shell, or attach to a running Herdr or tmux session on your server.

Get help

Email hi.moonlight@gmail.com, or use Settings → Report a Problem in the app. For feature ideas, use Suggest a Feature.

Include what happened, what you expected, and your app version. Device details are optional. Do not send passwords, private keys, passphrases, or private terminal output.

Connection and host verification

Check the host, port, username, network access, and server authentication settings. On a first connection, compare the host-key fingerprint with a trusted source. If a saved fingerprint changes, verify it with your server administrator before accepting it.

iCloud sync

Configuration sync uses your private iCloud database. Credential sync uses iCloud Keychain separately. Use the same iCloud account on both devices, enable Passwords & Keychain in system settings, then open Settings → iCloud Sync → Sync Now in MoonSSH. Configuration and credentials may arrive at different times; a synced host card alone does not confirm its credentials have arrived.

MoonSSH Pro

Free includes text and one image or file per message, with unlimited sends. Pro supports up to ten attachments per message through a monthly auto-renewing subscription. The purchase sheet shows the current App Store price for your region.

To restore an active purchase, use Settings → MoonSSH Pro → Restore Purchases with the same Apple Account. Manage or cancel your subscription in Apple Account subscription settings. Cancelling renewal keeps access through the paid period; it does not automatically refund the purchase. Apple handles billing and refund requests.

Background use

iOS can suspend network activity in the background. MoonSSH reconnects when possible; a remote Herdr or tmux process can continue on your server. Live Activities display status and provide a way back to the app; they do not keep an SSH connection running permanently.
