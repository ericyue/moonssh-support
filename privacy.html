Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026. Contact the developer at hi.moonlight@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests.

Your SSH servers and local data

MoonSSH connects directly from your device to the SSH servers you select. Host addresses, usernames, connection labels, trusted host fingerprints, interface preferences, and session restoration information are stored on your device. Passwords, private keys, and passphrases are stored in Keychain. There is no MoonSSH account requirement.

Terminal commands and files you choose to send go to the connected SSH server. Attachments are uploaded to that server under ~/.moonssh/uploads/. Server administrators control the storage, access, retention, and deletion of server-side data. Removing a local draft or saved host does not remove previously uploaded files from that server.

Optional iCloud configuration and credential sync

With configuration sync enabled, saved host details and selected preferences are synced through Apple's CloudKit private database using encrypted fields, associated with your iCloud account. Passwords, private keys, and passphrases are not included in that CloudKit payload. If credential sync is enabled, they sync separately through iCloud Keychain. Devices signed in to the same iCloud account may receive this data.

Trusted host fingerprints, active sessions, terminal output, and attachment contents are not part of configuration sync. You can turn configuration sync and credential sync off in Settings → iCloud Sync. Turning sync off keeps local copies and does not erase existing iCloud copies. Deleting a saved host while sync is enabled propagates the host deletion and associated credential deletion when the services are available. Offline or locked devices may complete changes later. Apple operates these services under its own privacy terms.

Purchases

Apple processes subscription purchases and payments. MoonSSH checks Apple-verified StoreKit transactions on your device to determine Pro access, including restoration, expiration, and revocation. MoonSSH does not receive your payment card details. For optional Pro hosted push, the app sends Apple-signed purchase and, when needed, renewal records to the MoonSSH Cloudflare relay to verify entitlement. Apple-signed server notifications update renewals, expiration and refunds without phone polling. Signed records may contain Apple transaction identifiers, product and purchase dates, storefront and subscription status. They are verified in memory and are not retained or logged. The relay stores a hash of the original purchase identifier, entitlement expiry, verification and notification timestamps, and its association with push pairings. This data is used only for app functionality and fraud prevention, is not used for advertising or tracking, and is deleted after 180 days without entitlement or verification activity. You can revoke individual push pairings in the app; minimal subscription verification state may remain during that retention period to prevent replay of refunded purchases. SSH credentials and attachments are never sent for subscription verification.

Photos, files, clipboard, and camera

MoonSSH accesses a camera, photo, file, or clipboard attachment when you choose the corresponding action. Selected content remains in your draft until you remove it or send it. Terminal snapshots, composer drafts, and draft attachment copies can be saved in the app’s local storage so a reopened session can show its previous screen and draft before reconnecting. These checkpoints use iOS complete file protection and are excluded from backups; they are not synced to iCloud or sent to the relay. Closing the session removes its checkpoint. A restored screen is a saved view: MoonSSH verifies the original pane before allowing input. A remote OSC 52 clipboard-write command can copy remote text to your local clipboard; remote clipboard-read requests are refused.

Voice input

When you start voice input, MoonSSH uses Apple’s on-device speech recognition. Microphone audio is processed locally while recording, is not saved as an audio file, and is not uploaded to MoonSSH or its relay. Recognized text is shown for your control and can be sent through your existing SSH connection when you finish input. The speech language preference can sync with your other settings through iCloud.

Notifications and Live Activities

If enabled, local notifications and Live Activities can show connection names and terminal or agent status summaries on your device, including on the lock screen. Control these features in MoonSSH and system notification settings. Their content can be visible to someone looking at your device.

Optional Herdr lock screen push

If you subscribe to Pro and pair the Herdr plugin, the configured relay receives an Apple push device token, an opaque pairing ID, and your notification preferences. Alerts include the event type, an opaque event ID, a hash used to find the original pane, and the recognized Agent type when available. Cloudflare runs the MoonSSH relay and Apple delivers the alert. Pairing requires a one-time Cloudflare Turnstile security check, with no MoonSSH account or invitation. Turnstile processes network and browser/device information to prevent automated abuse; see the Cloudflare Turnstile Privacy Policy. MoonSSH stores only daily salted hashes of the enrollment IP address, hashed device and proof identifiers, and registration time for rate limits and replay prevention, with a daily cleanup after one day. These records are used only for app functionality and security, never advertising or tracking.

Detailed push content is off by default. If you enable it, the plugin can include the Herdr task name or workspace and tab labels. For completed Codex and Claude Code turns, the plugin can also read a short reply excerpt from the matching local session on your SSH host. The plugin encrypts the task details and reply excerpt with your device’s public key. The relay and Apple Push Notification service receive ciphertext; the private key stays on the paired device. The plugin does not forward terminal buffers, prompts, or tool results as preview content. Once decrypted on your device, the preview may be visible on the lock screen according to your iOS notification settings. You can turn previews off per pairing in MoonSSH.

The relay stores the token and pairing while active, and automatically expires the pairing after 90 days without a registration refresh. A daily cleanup deletes deduplication records older than one day. Per-pairing and aggregate daily counters enforce service limits; old counters are removed by daily cleanup. Task labels and reply excerpts are not stored by the relay. Use Revoke Pairing in MoonSSH to remove the relay registration and invalidate the host key. Removing the plugin on your host stops new events from that host. The plugin maintains a private local outbox containing event metadata and, only when enabled, encrypted preview content. Transient delivery failures retry with backoff, at most nine attempts and for no longer than 15 minutes; expired events are discarded. Successful and revoked events are removed from the queue. No phone background polling is added. The plugin runs as the SSH user and is not sandboxed; review the published plugin source before installing. Host-side unpair deletes that Herdr session’s local pairing files and outbox; app-side Revoke Pairing removes the relay registration and invalidates its host key.

Feedback you send

Feedback is not sent automatically. You can review and edit a report, then send email, copy it, or share it through another app. Feedback emails sent to the support address disclose your sender address and the content you choose to send to the developer. Optional app and device details include the app version, build, system version, and device family. The app does not automatically attach SSH credentials, server details, terminal output, or diagnostic logs.

Feedback drafts remain in memory while the feedback screen is open. Copying places the report on your clipboard. Sharing passes it to the app and recipient you choose, whose policies then apply. Support correspondence is kept as needed to handle the request and maintain support records. You can request deletion of correspondence by emailing the support address; records required for legal obligations may be retained.

Usage statistics

Starting with MoonSSH 1.1.0 build 49, the app automatically collects coarse usage statistics to help the developer understand daily app use and feature adoption. The app sends coarse events such as foreground visits and duration, first-seen installations, and use of SSH, Herdr/tmux, voice input, the composer, attachments, push, terminal recovery, Live Activities, iCloud sync, interface controls and Pro purchase/restore flows to PostHog Cloud in the United States. These events use a random installation identifier with app version/build, operating-system version and device model/type. There is no MoonSSH account, email, Apple transaction identifier, advertising identifier or SSH identity associated with this analytics identifier. First seen means an installation's first participation in analytics; existing users upgrading to this version may be first seen then, and multiple devices are counted separately. It is not a count of App Store downloads.

Terminal contents, commands, prompts, replies, hostnames/IP addresses of SSH servers, usernames, keys, passwords, drafts, file names/contents and microphone audio or recognized speech are excluded. We do not enable session replay, automatic screen or element capture, automatic crash/log uploads, advertising or cross-app tracking. GeoIP enrichment is disabled. Like any HTTPS provider, PostHog processes request network metadata to deliver and protect its service. See the PostHog Privacy Policy.

Events are deduplicated per feature/control during a foreground visit, with a new UTC-day action eligible again; counts measure adoption rather than every key press. A bounded local SDK queue batches transmission. Analytics does not add SSH polling or a phone background execution service. This data is used only for product analytics, not advertising, and retained under the project's retention setting (currently up to one year on the free plan). You may contact the developer about deletion; because analytics is not associated with an account or email, the random analytics identifier may be needed to locate those events. Use Settings → Privacy & Data → Copy Statistics ID to provide it in a request.

This website

The developer does not operate a relay for your terminal traffic or an app database containing your SSH credentials. This support website uses GitHub Pages, which may process request information such as your IP address to serve and secure the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. These pages contain no added analytics scripts, advertising, or forms.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit or delete saved hosts and clear sensitive information you no longer need on your device. Deletion of synced hosts and credentials requires sync to be enabled and the corresponding Apple services to be available. Turning sync off alone does not delete cloud data. Uninstalling the app is not a reliable way to delete Keychain or iCloud copies. For data on your SSH server, remove it on that server or contact its administrator. Contact the developer for help with privacy requests relating to support correspondence.
