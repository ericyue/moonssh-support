Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026. Contact the developer at hi.moonlight@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests.

Your SSH servers and local data

MoonSSH connects directly from your device to the SSH servers you select. Host addresses, usernames, connection labels, trusted host fingerprints, interface preferences, and session restoration information are stored on your device. Passwords, private keys, and passphrases are stored in Keychain. There is no MoonSSH account requirement.

Terminal commands and files you choose to send go to the connected SSH server. Attachments are uploaded to that server under ~/.moonssh/uploads/. Server administrators control the storage, access, retention, and deletion of server-side data. Removing a local draft or saved host does not remove previously uploaded files from that server.

Optional iCloud configuration and credential sync

With configuration sync enabled, saved host details and selected preferences are synced through Apple's CloudKit private database using encrypted fields, associated with your iCloud account. Passwords, private keys, and passphrases are not included in that CloudKit payload. If credential sync is enabled, they sync separately through iCloud Keychain. Devices signed in to the same iCloud account may receive this data.

Trusted host fingerprints, active sessions, terminal output, and attachment contents are not part of configuration sync. You can turn configuration sync and credential sync off in Settings → iCloud Sync. Turning sync off keeps local copies and does not erase existing iCloud copies. Deleting a saved host while sync is enabled propagates the host deletion and associated credential deletion when the services are available. Offline or locked devices may complete changes later. Apple operates these services under its own privacy terms.

Purchases

Apple processes subscription purchases and payments. MoonSSH checks Apple-verified StoreKit transactions on your device to determine Pro access, including restoration, expiration, and revocation. MoonSSH does not receive your payment card details. The app does not send your SSH credentials or attachments to a developer-operated subscription server.

Photos, files, clipboard, and camera

MoonSSH accesses a camera, photo, file, or clipboard attachment when you choose the corresponding action. Selected content remains in your draft until you remove it or send it. Composer drafts are held in app memory while the session remains open; they are not cloud backups and can be lost when the process ends. A remote OSC 52 clipboard-write command can copy remote text to your local clipboard; remote clipboard-read requests are refused.

Notifications and Live Activities

If enabled, local notifications and Live Activities can show connection names and terminal or agent status summaries on your device, including on the lock screen. Control these features in MoonSSH and system notification settings. Their content can be visible to someone looking at your device.

Feedback you send

Feedback is not sent automatically. You can review and edit a report, then send email, copy it, or share it through another app. Feedback emails sent to the support address disclose your sender address and the content you choose to send to the developer. Optional app and device details include the app version, build, system version, and device family. The app does not automatically attach SSH credentials, server details, terminal output, or diagnostic logs.

Feedback drafts remain in memory while the feedback screen is open. Copying places the report on your clipboard. Sharing passes it to the app and recipient you choose, whose policies then apply. Support correspondence is kept as needed to handle the request and maintain support records. You can request deletion of correspondence by emailing the support address; records required for legal obligations may be retained.

Analytics, tracking, and this website

The app does not contain developer-operated advertising, tracking, or automatic diagnostic upload services. The developer does not operate a relay for your terminal traffic or an app database containing your SSH credentials. This support website uses GitHub Pages, which may process request information such as your IP address to serve and secure the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. These pages contain no added analytics scripts, advertising, or forms.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit or delete saved hosts and clear sensitive information you no longer need on your device. Deletion of synced hosts and credentials requires sync to be enabled and the corresponding Apple services to be available. Turning sync off alone does not delete cloud data. Uninstalling the app is not a reliable way to delete Keychain or iCloud copies. For data on your SSH server, remove it on that server or contact its administrator. Contact the developer for help with privacy requests relating to support correspondence.

Changes

This policy may change when the app's data practices change. The current policy and its effective date will be available on this page.
